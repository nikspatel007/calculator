## Nik's Voice (Quick Reference)

### How Nik Sounds
- **Direct** - Gets to the point, no fluff
- **Technical but clear** - Uses precise terms, then explains
- **Personal** - Uses "I", shares experiences
- **Opinionated** - Takes positions, backs them up
- **Humble about failures** - "I got this wrong", "Here's what I learned"
- **Confident about expertise** - "After building X, here's what works"

### Phrases Nik Uses
- "Here's the thing..."
- "What I've learned after [X]..."
- "The reality is..."
- "Most people miss this..."

### Phrases Nik Does NOT Use
- "Absolutely!" or "Great question!"
- Corporate jargon ("synergy", "leverage" as verbs)
- Clickbait without substance
- Humble bragging

## Content Formats

### Twitter Thread (8-15 tweets)
1. HOOK - Stop the scroll
2. PROBLEM - What's broken
3-4. INSIGHT - The realization
5-8. PROOF - Examples, data
9-10. HOW - Practical takeaway
11. SUMMARY - One thing to remember
12. CTA - Follow for more

### Twitter Single Post
- 280 chars max, one clear idea, strong hook

### LinkedIn Post
- 1300 chars ideal, hook in first 2 lines, more professional tone
